How a take is made

Window follows you

Window mode records just that window, even when it's moved or covered by another one.

Area is exact

Area mode records the display cropped to your rectangle (sourceRect). Video sides are rounded to even pixels so HEVC accepts them.

Clicks line up

events.json now stores positions relative to what was recorded, so later zooms land in the right place in every mode.

Next: the camera bubble

Proposed
Safari
While recording. A round camera preview in your Version One ring floats over your screen. It's off by default, with a Camera switch next to Mic.

The camera saves to its own camera.mov in the take folder rather than being burned into the screen video. In the editor you can then move, resize or hide the bubble, or go full-camera for a section. Screen Studio and Tella work the same way.

Until export is built, a take has the screen and camera as two files side by side.
