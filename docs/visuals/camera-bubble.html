One switch

Camera on shows the bubble and records camera.mov. Off hides it and turns the camera light off. It's saved between launches.

Which camera

Automatic follows macOS, so an iPhone on a stand (Continuity Camera) takes over by itself. Or pick one: your iPhone, its Desk View, the Mac's camera, or a webcam.

Access

The first time, macOS asks for Camera access. Get set up now has a Camera row, marked optional, so you can record without it.

How it lines up

Its own file

Not burned into the screen video, the way Screen Studio and Tella work. So the editor can move it, hide it, or go full camera for a section.

Places, not pixels

Bubble spots are saved as shares of what was recorded, so they land right whether you recorded the screen, a window or an area.

Crash safe

camera.mov is written in 2 second pieces, like screen.mov, so a crash loses a few seconds at most.

In the export

Exported. camera.mov is drawn as a circle in your ring, at the spot you left it, the same share of the frame as on your screen.

Follows your moves

If you dragged the bubble while recording, it jumps to each new spot at the moment you moved it.

Cut with the screen

The camera is cut exactly where the screen is, using camera.json's offset, so your lips stay in sync after every cut.

Not in clips yet

Short 9:16 and 1:1 clips leave the bubble out for now, since their crop would slice it.
