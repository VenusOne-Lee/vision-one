Just the camera

A fourth way to record, next to Screen, Window and Area. Camera only records you talking to the camera and your mic, with nothing from the screen. It doesn't need Screen Recording access at all, just Camera and Microphone.

Same place as the other modes

Everyone puts it beside the screen choices, not in a separate app. So it's a fourth segment, and a pick on Home's record button.

A big mirrored preview

The bubble is too small to frame yourself. You get a resizable 16:9 window that reads like a mirror. The saved file isn't mirrored, so text behind you reads the right way.

Camera is the main video

The camera and mic go into the take's main movie, so the transcript editor, captions, cuts, clips and export all work with no changes.

No screen permission

Camera only never touches ScreenCaptureKit. If you've never turned on Screen Recording, you can still record yourself today.

How it works

  • Defaults: 1080p if the camera has it, otherwise 720p. Landscape 16:9. Mic on, system sound off.
  • Camera access is asked for the first time you record this way. If it was turned off, the message says where to turn it back on.
  • Bubble: hidden while Camera only records, since the camera is already the whole picture.
  • The stage opens when you press record, so you can frame yourself during the countdown. It isn't recorded; the camera is.
